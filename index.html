A note on respect

Most of the vocabulary here originates in African-American Vernacular English (AAVE), and in specific scenes and cities — hip-hop, ballroom culture, London's Multicultural English, Jamaican Patois. This project documents and credits that language; it doesn't costume it. Definitions are written the way a good dictionary writes them, origins are named on every entry, and contributions that mock rather than explain are declined.
